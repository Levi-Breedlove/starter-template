## Acceptance decision

The ten scenario-level rubric rows have supporting observations. The separate mandatory G4 handoff and structured closeout failed. The retained native traces show two initial lookup errors in the replacement turn and a completed local workflow-validation receipt before timeout. A saved G2 handoff would lead a returning developer to work already completed. Local passing tests cannot establish a successful native closeout.

The observed implementation and tests can be inspected without another model call. Further native execution requires a new allocation and a reviewed starting point. Any future attempt must preserve this timeout and its evidence. The observations cover the frozen source and prompts. Final distribution startup with literal `start`, emulator runtime, and live AWS delivery require separate evidence.
